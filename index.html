CALL THE SALON ↗
01 Is Heavens a unisex salon?+

Yes. Heavens Hair & Beauty is a unisex salon, welcoming guests looking for hair, grooming and beauty services.

02 How can I check service prices?+

Service needs can vary, so please call the salon to confirm the latest service options and pricing before your visit.

03 Can I ask about a specific hairstyle?+

Absolutely. You can bring a reference photo or describe the look you have in mind. The salon team can guide you on suitable options.

04 Where is the salon located?+

Heavens is at Senara Bazar, near the BSNL Office. Use the contact details below to confirm directions before setting out.

05 Should I call before visiting?+

Calling ahead is a good idea, especially if you have a particular service or timing in mind. The team can let you know the best time to come in.
